The Filter & The Seal

Filtration (eGFR)

Think of your kidneys as a sieve. A low eGFR means the sieve is clogged—waste products like creatinine are staying in the blood instead of moving into the urine.

Permeability (Albumin)

Albuminuria means the sieve has holes. Healthy kidneys keep large protein molecules in the blood. If they leak into urine, the "seal" of the kidney is failing.

Clinical Note: A patient can have a normal eGFR (90+) but still have significant Kidney Disease if Albuminuria is present.
