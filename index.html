The Filter & The Seal

Filtration (eGFR)

Think of your kidneys as a sieve. A low eGFR means the sieve is clogged—waste products like creatinine stay in the blood instead of moving to urine.

Permeability (Albumin)

Albuminuria means the sieve has holes. Healthy kidneys keep protein in the blood. If they leak into urine, the "seal" of the kidney is failing.

Clinical Note: A patient can have a normal eGFR (90+) but still have significant disease if Albuminuria is present.

Who Should Test?

Diabetes High Risk

High blood sugar damages small vessels in the kidney over time.

Hypertension High Risk

High pressure scars the kidney's delicate filtration units.

Family History Moderate Risk

Genetic predispositions like Polycystic Kidney Disease.

Red Flag Symptoms

Foamy urine, swelling in ankles (edema), fatigue, or unexplained itching.
